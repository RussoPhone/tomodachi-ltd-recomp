# Getting help

- **Something failed?** Run `./play.sh --nogui --diagnose`, then read [docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
- **Still stuck?** Open an [issue](https://github.com/RussoPhone/tomodachi-ltd-recomp/issues/new/choose) and attach the report from
  `./install.sh --report` (installing) or `local/logs/play-nxrt-*.log` (playing). They contain no game data or keys.
- **Tried it on your hardware?** Tell us with a [compatibility report](docs/COMPATIBILITY.md): it helps even when everything works.
- **Please do not** post keys, firmware, the `.nsp` or any copy of the game, and do not ask for them.

This is a fan project made by one person with AI assistance; answers may take a while. See the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP-RECOMPILER.md) for where it is going.
